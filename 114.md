# #114 Introducing Party Race gamemode - place obstacles to bamboozle the friends, or put windy Fans to reach secret Stars and Finish first. Inspired by Ultimate Chicken Horse. Checkout Free in our Demo!

https://youtu.be/-h72OfcKwRo

On round start - choose item from randomized box. Someone might take item before you clicked on it. 60 seconds to choose, and find place where you wanna put the obstacle. Rotation and resizing gizmo provides more freedom for advanced traps or simplified paths.
Reach Finish first to earn more stars. Wins who has most Stars after 5 rounds. Collect secret stars on level, or players can choose Star item to place.
