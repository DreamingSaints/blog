# #111 Ball-Aqua: bad reviews

Collection of constructive criticism and lessons learned

- Q: "This game has one flaw: the coloring is off. The UI design relies heavily on transparent and pale colors, while the in-game environments are extremely vibrant—like the bright green grass. This creates a harsh visual contrast that is very uncomfortable to look at. The official level design is decent; I even played a 'Heart Cube' diving-type level. I suggest polishing the visual details, as games of this genre generally rely on three key elements: creativity, graphics, and controls."
- A: Our Solution: darker background, extra background behind text, saturated darkened buttons, bright text, dark overlay around main window
