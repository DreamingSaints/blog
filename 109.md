# #109 We released multiplayer frutiger-aero tropical platformer: Ball-Aqua Demo, try it

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T9NxPA8DNJE

Ball-Aqua is a physics ball platformer on tropical courses: fragile glass, curvy rails, pushing fans, bumpy sand, and deadly water. Roll, jump, and learn the inertia. Pop easy bubbles, hunt challenging stars, beat the timer. A 100% clear unlocks skins.

Freeroam is solo or co-op. Each player tracks shared or own collectibles (toggleable); next level, when everyone has finished. Race is the other mode: each round everyone places one obstacle - ramp, wall, or trap - then you run the remixed track (Ultimate Chicken Horse inspired). First to finish wins.

The campaign teaches the moves. A level editor and skin editor ship with Steam Workshop, so you can build courses, customize balls, and subscribe to community maps. Liquid Glass UI, Frutiger Aero grass and ocean. Use keyboard + mouse, or gamepad. The free demo is 15 levels in solo or with friends.
