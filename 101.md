# 101 Ball-Aqua: multiplayer tropical ball-rolling platforming

*[Ball-Aqua](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4967660/BallAqua/) by [Dreaming Saints](https://dreamingsaints.github.io/blog/)* (Demo Launch on August 20!)

Inspired by old **[Ballance](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2000770/Ballance/)** and recent watery music **[alyzea - dream island](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3R6beuJQkxM)**

> Roll a physics ball through tropical obstacle courses. Beat the timer, collect bubbles and stars, and chase 100% clears. Play Freeroam solo or co-op, compete in Race, build levels, and share on Steam Workshop.

I saw a lot of ball-rolling games recently developed, but I desired more than  I see in them. I need co-op with friends, and real ball physics for fast roll and precise jumps. Some rails. 

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0PRSHjpn2RY

> ## 1. **Roll** your ball through **obstacle courses**
> 
> Steer a **physics ball** across suspended **rails**, **spinning fans**, fragile **glass**, and more. **Roll** fast, **jump** gaps, and master the **inertia**.
> 
> Pop easy **bubbles**, reach challenging **stars**. Beat the target **time** and grab every collectible for a **100%** clear to unlock **skins** for your ball.
> 
> ## 2. **Campaign**, custom levels, and Workshop
> 
> * **Campaign**: Hand-crafted courses that teach and challenge the core mechanics. Clear a stage to unlock the next.
> 
> * **Custom**: Save and play levels you build in the Level Editor. Share them on Workshop.
> 
> * **Workshop**: Subscribe to community tracks and browse the in-game catalog.
> 
> ## 3. **Freeroam** and **Race**
> 
> **Host** or join open Steam **lobbies**. Invite **friends**. Play **campaign** or **custom** levels together - or race them.
> 
> ## 3.1 **Freeroam**
> 
> Finish levels **solo** or in **co-op** (up to 99 players). Roll the course, collect **stars** and **bubbles**, set **checkpoints**, and reach the finish. Each player tracks their own collectibles - when everyone has finished at least once, the host can move the group to the next course. Practice routes, explore Workshop maps, or unwind before Race.
> 
> ## 3.2 **Race**
> 
> Each round, every player picks and places one **building piece** - a helpful ramp, a nasty wall, or a watery trap. Help yourself or block the pack, then race the course you just remixed. First to finish earns the **stars**.
> 
> ## 4. **Level editor**
> 
> Build courses: place rails, floors, props, glass, fans, and collectibles on an optional grid. Fly the camera, multi-select, rotate, duplicate, and **playtest** instantly. Set your own target time, then export and publish to Steam Workshop - with auto-generated preview images.
> 
> ## 5. **Skin editor**
> 
> Paint custom ball skins with your own colors and textures, then save them for play or share on Steam Workshop.
> 
> ## 6. **Frutiger Aero** grass and ocean beaches
> 
> Bright grass, warm sand, and cool glass under open sky. Flowers, fish, and butterflies drift by as you roll - sunny, watery, and a little nostalgic.
> 
> ## 7. **Features**
> 
> * Unlockable and custom ball skins, plus Steam Workshop sharing
> 
> * Keyboard, mouse, and gamepad with full rebinding
> 
> * 31 languages supported
> 
> * Tactile audio with separate master, music, and SFX volume
> 
> * Water ripples, inertia squash-and-stretch, and ground particles
> 
> * Liquid Glass UI with real frosted backdrop blur
> 
> * Graphics settings: bloom, shadows, TAA, V-Sync, FPS cap, window modes, resolution
> 
> * Optional ray tracing on DirectX 12, including colored shadows
